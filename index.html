06 / RULES & CONTRACTS

Before you mint

Agent allowance

Each Agent receives 2 free and 4 paid mint allocations. Maximum 6 total, shared across up to 2 linked wallets.

Mint price & gas

Paid mint price is $1 worth of BNB, calculated from a trusted BNB/USD price feed. Agent pays network gas separately.

Rarity tiers

Common 70%, Rare 25%, Legendary 5%. Exact collection quotas require secure on-chain allocation logic.

Security & randomness

Planned EIP-712 signed vouchers, nonce and expiry protection, and Chainlink VRF v2.5. These are not active in this frontend demo.
